About

Our team.

A small, stroke-focused team led by Cade McMillan, who reviews every referral personally.

Founder

Cade McMillan, founder & director.

Cade has several years of hands-on experience in disability and community support in Queensland. He leads every referral personally and is the named contact for families, hospital teams and support coordinators.

We support NDIS participants whose plans are plan-managed or self-managed.

Our workers

How we choose and prepare support workers.

  • Workers hold or obtain a current NDIS Worker Screening Check and a Working with Children Check, where required.
  • Every worker completes a stroke-specific induction before their first shift, covering aphasia, swallowing, fatigue, one-sided weakness and falls.
  • Workers are introduced before they start, and matches are changed if they are not working.
  • The person's therapy plan, swallowing plan and communication approach are shared before the first shift.
