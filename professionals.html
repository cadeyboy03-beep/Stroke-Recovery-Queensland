Referring a patient after stroke.

A stroke-focused in-home support service for plan-managed and self-managed NDIS participants, built to make a referral easy and to keep you informed.

Call 0450 521 274
Why a stroke specialist

Why refer to a stroke-focused service.

Stroke-specific induction

Workers learn about aphasia, swallowing plans, fatigue and one-sided weakness before the first shift.

Therapy goes home

Exercise and communication programs are practised between appointments, with feedback to you.

Discharge-ready

A worker introduced before discharge and in place on day one.

How a referral works

Four steps, one named contact.

1. Contact

Send a referral or call. No NDIS number needed to ask a question.

2. Intake

We confirm the plan type and funding and reply to you within 24 hours.

3. Outreach

We contact the person and family, and the ward team if appropriate.

4. Updates

You hear from us about the start date and progress, with consent.

Questions

Referrer questions.

Does the patient need an NDIS plan?

To start support, yes, and it must be plan-managed or self-managed. You can call with a question at any time.

What about patients who turn 65?

NDIS participants who entered the scheme before 65 can usually stay. People who are not NDIS participants at 65 are generally directed to aged care.

Do you cover regional areas?

We currently support people in Moreton Bay (north Brisbane), the Sunshine Coast, the Gold Coast, Hervey Bay and Bundaberg. If you live elsewhere, call and we will tell you honestly whether we can help or who might.

How soon can support start?

It depends on rosters and the worker match. We agree a date with the family and discharge team as early as possible.

What if I am not sure the patient is eligible?

Call us. We will help you work out the next step.
