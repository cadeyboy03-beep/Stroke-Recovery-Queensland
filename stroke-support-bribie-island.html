Close to home

Specialist stroke support in Bribie Island.

Bribie Island is a popular retirement community, and many residents live without family nearby. After a stroke, getting off the island to appointments and staying connected locally both take planning, and we build that into the roster.

We take referrals from Bribie Island and the surrounding area now. Send a referral or call 0450 521 274, and we will reply within 24 hours and tell you plainly what we can arrange, and when.

Hospital to home

From hospital or rehab to home in Bribie Island.

Hospitals and services people in this area commonly use include Caboolture Hospital, Redcliffe Hospital, The Prince Charles Hospital (Chermside). We are independent of these services. We work alongside the treating team, the support coordinator and family to plan a safe return home.

If someone you care about is still on the ward or in rehab, the earlier we hear, the better. A worker can meet them before discharge, learn their routines and be there on the first day home.

Questions

Stroke support in Bribie Island: common questions

Do you support people in Bribie Island who are leaving hospital after a stroke?

Yes. Early contact helps: we can speak with the person, the family and the ward team before discharge and plan a first-week roster. Call 0450 521 274 or send a referral and we reply within 24 hours.

Can I use my NDIS funding for stroke support in Bribie Island?

If your plan is plan-managed or self-managed and includes funding for daily living or community supports, yes. We are not NDIS-registered, so we cannot support NDIA-managed participants. Your plan manager or support coordinator can confirm what your plan covers.

How many people near Bribie Island have a stroke and use the NDIS?

As at 30 June 2026 there were 262 NDIS participants in the Caboolture/Strathpine service district with stroke as their primary disability, according to NDIA data. The district is larger than one town, and many more people live with stroke without being NDIS participants.

Do you travel to outlying towns around Bribie Island?

Often yes. Tell us the address when you call and we will confirm what is practical, including travel time and any charges, before anything starts. Travel charges, where they apply, are set out in the service agreement.

How quickly can support start?

It depends on rosters and the worker match. We reply to referrals within 24 hours and agree a start date with the family and the discharge team as early as possible.
